Climate Adaptation

Multan Climate & Seasonal Survival

Arid desert-fringe climate, scorching summer temperatures (up to 49°C), high solar radiation, low humidity. Roadside unhardened plants fail easily in Multan's extreme weather cycles. Hamari nursery flora Multan ke temperature and seasonal stress ke liye root-hardened hoti hai.

Recommended Turf Grass:

Tifway 419 Bermuda Grass (Survives intense heat and low moisture).

Soil Bio-Chemistry

Multan Soil Conditioning Formula

Alluvial river silt combined with vermicompost and hydrogel moisture-retention crystals. Raw soil testing ensures high fertility, moisture conservation, and deep root anchorage without compaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Multan Landscaping FAQs

Q. Multan ki 49°C garmi me paude jalte kyu hain aur bachao kese ho?
Multan me direct dhoop se bachane ke liye hum pehle canopy shade trees lagate hain aur root zone par 3-inch thick organic mulch dalte hain jo mitti ka temperature 8°C tak thanda rakhta hai.
Q. DHA Multan me automated drip system ka kia faida hai?
Multan me paani dhoop se jaldi evaporate hota hai. Automated drip system subah sawere roots ko direct paani deta hai jis se 60% paani bachta hai aur pauda kabhi nahi murjhata.
