National Sustainability Initiative

Green Pakistan: Free Plants for a Sustainable Future

The Living Garden provides free certified plants to schools, colleges, universities, hospitals, government buildings, community centers, and rural belts to create a cleaner, greener Pakistan.

🍃
Oxygen & Air Quality

Plants filter harmful toxic PM2.5 smog pollutants and generate continuous fresh oxygen in urban density centers.

🌍
Carbon Sequestration

Trees act as permanent natural carbon sinks, mitigating global climate change and lowering ambient temperatures by 3-5°C.

🏥
Hospital Healing Sanctuaries

Green healing gardens in hospitals reduce patient stress, lower blood pressure, and accelerate convalescence recovery.

💼
Green Job Creation

Direct employment for local nursery gardeners, transport drivers, logistics handlers, and eco-agriculture workers.
