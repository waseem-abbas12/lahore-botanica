Lahore Climate Plant Survival Guide

Garmiyon ki 'Loo' aur sardiyon ki 'Smog' se paudon ko bachane ke aasan tareeqay.

🔥

Summer Heatwaves (May - July)

Dopehar ki dhoop (12 PM se 4 PM) ke waqt indoor plants ko khirki se 3 feet door rakhein. Paani hamesha subah 7 AM se pehle ya maghrib ke baad dein.

🌫️

Smog & Dust Protection (Oct - Dec)

Haftay mein ek martaba foliage (patton) ko fresh paani se spray karein taake dhoop absorb karne ke pores khulay rahein aur smog ka asar na ho.

🪱

Organic Lahore Soil Mix (Bhal Mitti)

Hamare tmam pauday canal silt (Bhal mitti), cow-dung compost aur perlite ke balanced ratio me lagaye gaye hain jo jaron ko rot nahi hone dete.
