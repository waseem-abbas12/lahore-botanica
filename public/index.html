Horticulture Science

Lahore Climate Plant Survival Guide

Lahore ke mausam me 47°C ki shadeed garmi aur November ki zahreeli smog se paudon ko taza aur sar-sabz rakhne ke certified tareeqay.

🔥

Summer Heatwaves (May - July)

Dopehar ki dhoop (12 PM se 4 PM) ke waqt indoor plants ko khirki se 3 feet door rakhein. Paani hamesha subah 7 AM se pehle ya maghrib ke baad dein. Root burn se bachane ke liye mulch istemal karein.

🌫️

Smog & Dust Protection (Oct - Dec)

Haftay mein ek martaba foliage (patton) ko fresh paani se spray karein taake dhoop absorb karne ke pores khulay rahein aur smog ka toxic PM2.5 asar na ho. Indoor air purifier plants room me rakhein.

🪱

Organic Lahore Soil Mix (Bhal Mitti)

Hamare tmam pauday canal silt (Bhal mitti), cow-dung compost aur perlite ke balanced ratio me lagaye gaye hain jo jaron ko rot nahi hone dete aur dhoop me mitti ko sakht pathar nahi banate.
