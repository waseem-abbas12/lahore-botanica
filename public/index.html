FAQ

Common questions

Do you charge for the site visit?

The first site visit and consultation within our service cities is free. We assess the site, soil, sunlight and drainage, and discuss your budget.

How is the landscaping cost calculated?

Cost depends on area, grass type, trees and palms, hardscape, water features, irrigation and lighting. After the site visit you receive an itemised quotation (BOQ). The estimator above gives an early indicative range.

How long does a project take?

A typical home lawn takes one to three weeks. Farmhouses, societies and commercial sites are scheduled in phases. The timeline is written into your quotation.

Which grass is best for Lahore and Punjab?

American Bermuda handles heat and foot traffic well; Dhaka grass is popular for its soft look; Zoysia is denser and more premium. We recommend based on sunlight, water and use.

Do you work outside Lahore?

Yes. We take projects in Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Karachi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Sialkot and Gujranwala, and larger projects elsewhere in Pakistan.

Do you offer maintenance after handover?

Yes. Maintenance plans include scheduled gardener visits for mowing, pruning, fertilising, pest control and irrigation checks.
