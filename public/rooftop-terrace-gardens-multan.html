Rooftop Gardens Built for Multan

Multan is one of the hottest cities in Pakistan, with long, very hot summers, dust storms and little rain. Plants must be heat-hardy, and lawns need careful watering and some shade.

Evaporation is extreme in summer, so drip lines, mulch, timers and smaller lawn areas make a big difference. Water quality varies and should be checked before installation.

Chhat par garden ka sab se bara dar seelan (seepage) hai. A rooftop garden is safe only when waterproofing, drainage and load are planned first, and plants come last.

Pricing: Modern rooftop packages range from Rs. 65,000 to 150,000 depending on size and finishes. Waterproofing is quoted separately if needed.
Climate: Arid desert-fringe climate, scorching summer temperatures (up to 49°C), high solar radiation, low humidity.
Recommended turf: Tifway 419 Bermuda Grass (Survives intense heat and low moisture).
Popular plants: Ajwa & Aseel Date Palms, Moringa Oleifera, Gulmohar (Delonix regia), Kikar / Acacia, Bougainvillea, Aloe & Agave Succulents

Multan Tips

  • ✦Choose Bermuda or Paspalum for full-sun lawns
  • ✦Plant windbreak hedges against dust storms
  • ✦Water before sunrise, never at midday

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide rooftop gardens in Multan?+

Yes. The Living Garden provides rooftop & terrace gardens in Multan, including DHA Multan, Bosan Road, Gulgasht Colony, Cantt, Wapda Town, Royal Orchard and Buch Villas. Book a free site visit to get a plan and an itemised quote.

What should I keep in mind for Multan's climate?+

Multan is one of the hottest cities in Pakistan, with long, very hot summers, dust storms and little rain. Plants must be heat-hardy, and lawns need careful watering and some shade. Evaporation is extreme in summer, so drip lines, mulch, timers and smaller lawn areas make a big difference. Water quality varies and should be checked before installation.

Kya rooftop garden se chhat kharab hoti hai?+

Agar waterproofing, drainage aur root barrier sahi lage hon to nahi. Hum planting se pehle flood test karte hain taake koi leak pehle hi pakra jaye.

Can I have real grass on the roof?+

Yes, on a properly waterproofed roof with drainage and enough soil depth. Many clients prefer a mix: planters and artificial grass on the main area to keep weight and water use low.

Multan ki 49°C garmi me paude jalte kyu hain aur bachao kese ho?+

Multan me direct dhoop se bachane ke liye hum pehle canopy shade trees lagate hain aur root zone par 3-inch thick organic mulch dalte hain jo mitti ka temperature 8°C tak thanda rakhta hai.
