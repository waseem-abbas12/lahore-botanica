Part 1

Getting started

What does The Living Garden do?+

We are a landscape design and build company. We design and build complete gardens: lawns, planting, hardscape, pergolas, water features, irrigation, lighting, rooftops and vertical gardens, and we maintain them. We work on projects, not single plant sales.

Which cities do you serve?+

We are based in Lahore and take projects across Pakistan, including Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Karachi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Sialkot and Gujranwala. Larger projects in other cities are also welcome.

Site visit free hai?+

Ji haan. Pehli site visit aur consultation free hai. Hum naap lete hain, mitti, dhoop aur paani check karte hain, aur phir itemised quotation dete hain.

How do I book a site visit?+

Fill the free site visit form on any page, or message us on WhatsApp with your location, plot size and what you have in mind.

Do I need a design before building?+

For small front lawns it is optional. For 1 kanal and larger homes, farmhouses, rooftops and commercial sites, a design saves money by fixing levels, drainage and pipe routes first.

Part 2

Cost & timelines

How much does landscaping cost in Pakistan?+

Indicative packages: 5 to 10 marla front lawn Rs. 45,000 to 85,000; 1 kanal luxury garden Rs. 140,000 to 260,000; modern rooftop Rs. 65,000 to 150,000. Farmhouses and commercial sites are quoted after a site visit.

What is the price of grass per square foot?+

Our indicative installed rates are about Rs. 45 per sq ft for American Bermuda, Rs. 55 for Fine Dhaka and Rs. 75 for Royal Zoysia (Korean), including standard soil preparation. Heavy debris removal or extra soil is quoted separately.

How long does a garden project take?+

A typical home lawn takes days to a few weeks depending on soil work and hardscape. Farmhouses and commercial projects are planned in phases. You get a timeline with the quote.

Do you take advance payment?+

Payment terms are shared with the quotation, usually staged against work milestones.

Kya kaam phases me ho sakta hai?+

Bilkul. Hum pehle poora plan banate hain, phir budget ke hisab se phases me kaam karte hain, jaise pehle soil, irrigation aur grass, baad me pergola ya lighting.

Part 3

Grass & lawns

Which grass is best for Pakistan?+

American Bermuda for full sun and heavy use, Fine Dhaka for a soft formal front lawn, Zoysia (Korean) for a dense low-mowing carpet with some shade tolerance, and Paspalum or salt-tolerant Bermuda for salty coastal soil.

When is the best time to lay a lawn?+

Late February to April, and again from late summer to early autumn. Avoid the coldest months.

Why does my lawn turn yellow in winter?+

Warm-season grasses go dormant in the cold and lose colour. They green up again in spring. Reduce watering in winter.

Grass shade me kyun nahi lagti?+

Zyada tar lawn grasses ko roz 6 ghante ya zyada dhoop chahiye. Ghani chhaon me ground cover, gravel ya paving behtar option hai.

Do you install artificial grass?+

Yes, mainly on rooftops, balconies and spots where real grass cannot grow. We recommend real grass wherever it can thrive.

Part 4

Rooftops, irrigation & features

Will a rooftop garden cause seepage?+

Not when built properly. We check the slab, apply waterproofing, run a flood test, and add drainage and a root barrier before planting.

Drip or sprinkler, which is better?+

Sprinklers for lawns, drip for beds, hedges, trees and planters. Most gardens use both on separate timed zones.

Do water features waste water?+

No. Our fountains and rockeries recirculate the same water. You only top up what evaporates.

Do vertical gardens damage walls?+

A proper system keeps a gap from the wall and has a drainage tray, so water does not reach the wall.

Can you add lighting to an existing garden?+

Yes. We plan cable routes to avoid damaging the lawn and use weatherproof LED fittings on timers.

Part 5

Maintenance & aftercare

Do you offer maintenance contracts?+

Yes, monthly plans for homes and annual contracts for commercial sites and societies, covering mowing, feeding, pruning, pest control and irrigation checks.

Grass kitni dafa kaatni chahiye?+

Garmi aur barsaat me aam tor par hafte me ek baar, sardi me kam. Ek waqt me patti ka ek tihai se zyada na kaatein.

How do I protect plants from smog?+

Spray leaves with plain water every few days in smog season, water in the morning, and avoid feeding stressed plants.

What if plants die after handover?+

Aftercare terms are written into the quotation. Our maintenance plans include replacing failed plants as part of the service.

Do you work on gardens built by others?+

Yes. We assess the garden, fix urgent problems such as drainage or patchy grass, and then move to regular care.
