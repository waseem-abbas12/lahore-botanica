Lahore Climate & Seasonal Survival

Hot summers (45-47°C), heavy winter smog (PM2.5), fertile canal-silt soil. Roadside unhardened plants fail easily in Lahore's extreme weather cycles. Hamari nursery flora Lahore ke temperature and seasonal stress ke liye root-hardened hoti hai.

Recommended Turf Grass:

Fine Bermuda Carpet Grass & Imported American Turf (Heat and drought hardy).

Soil Bio-Chemistry

Lahore Soil Conditioning Formula

Pure Ravi/Canal-silt Bhal Mitti enriched with balanced organic cow compost & perlite. Raw soil testing ensures high fertility, moisture conservation, and deep root anchorage without compaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Lahore Landscaping FAQs

Q. Lahore ki garmi aur dhoop me konsi grass sab se behtar rehti hai?
Bermuda (American) Carpet Grass Lahore ke 47°C garmi ke mausam ke liye sab se best hai. Ye direct dhoop me tezi se phailti hai aur paani ki munasib miqdar se bilkul sar-sabz rehti hai.
Q. Lahore me site inspection kab ho sakti hai?
Lahore me hamare architects DHA, Gulberg, Lake City aur Bedian belt par 2 ghante ke andar site visit kar ke 3D master plan aur quotation faraham karte hain.
Q. DHA Lahore ke villas ke liye Bhal Mitti kahan se aati hai?
Hamari Bhal mitti certified canal-silt silt hoti hai jo Ravi canal belts se balance kar ke organic compost ke sath blend ki jati hai ta k paudon ki roots mazboot hon.
