Villa & Farmhouse Landscaping Built for Islamabad

Islamabad has a milder climate with real winters, frequent rain and some frost in colder months. Soil in many sectors is rocky or clay-heavy, and plots on slopes need careful leveling and drainage.

Rainfall is higher than in the plains, so drainage matters as much as irrigation. Boring water quality varies by sector and should be checked before installing drip lines.

Kanal homes and farmhouses need more than grass. They need a plan for driveways, guest seating, a family lawn, privacy along the boundary, shade, and water that reaches every corner.

Pricing: 1 kanal luxury garden packages start from Rs. 140,000 to 260,000. Farmhouses are quoted after a site visit because scope varies a lot.
Climate: Subtropical highland, January frost (0-2°C), heavy monsoon rains, rocky Potohar soil contours.
Recommended turf: Fine Fescue & Korean Grass blend for frost resistance; Bermuda for full-sun sectors.
Popular plants: Chir Pine (Pinus roxburghii), Cypress Conifers, Weeping Willow, Silver Oak, Hydrangea, Wisteria Creepers

Islamabad Tips

  • ✦Choose grasses and plants that handle winter cold and frost
  • ✦Build proper drainage on sloped and rocky plots
  • ✦Use the spring season for most new planting

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide villa & farmhouse landscaping in Islamabad?+

Yes. The Living Garden provides villa & farmhouse landscaping in Islamabad, including F, E and G sectors, DHA and Bahria Town Islamabad, Gulberg Greens, B-17, Park Road and farmhouses around Chak Shahzad. Book a free site visit to get a plan and an itemised quote.

What should I keep in mind for Islamabad's climate?+

Islamabad has a milder climate with real winters, frequent rain and some frost in colder months. Soil in many sectors is rocky or clay-heavy, and plots on slopes need careful leveling and drainage. Rainfall is higher than in the plains, so drainage matters as much as irrigation. Boring water quality varies by sector and should be checked before installing drip lines.

Farmhouse landscaping me kitna waqt lagta hai?+

Scope par depend karta hai. Ek 1 kanal ghar ka garden aam tor par kuch hafton me mukammal ho jata hai. Bari farmhouses ko phases me kiya jata hai, taake har hissa mausam ke hisab se lagaya ja sake.

Can you work on a farmhouse outside Lahore?+

Yes. We take projects across Pakistan. For distant sites, the team plans material delivery and stays on site for the build phases.

Kia Islamabad ki sardi aur kora (frost) paudon ko kharab karta hai?+

Ji haan, Islamabad me January ke koray se naazuk tropical paude mutasir hote hain. Hum Islamabad ke liye certified frost-hardy plants (Conifers, Pines, Boxwood, Silver Oak) aur root mulching provide karte hain.
