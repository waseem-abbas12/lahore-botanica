Climate Adaptation

Rawalpindi Climate & Seasonal Survival

Potohar continental, hot pre-monsoon, cool winters, undulating red clay terrain. Roadside unhardened plants fail easily in Rawalpindi's extreme weather cycles. Hamari nursery flora Rawalpindi ke temperature and seasonal stress ke liye root-hardened hoti hai.

Recommended Turf Grass:

Imported Bermuda Turf & Fine Turf with proper topsoil dressing.

Soil Bio-Chemistry

Rawalpindi Soil Conditioning Formula

Deep soil tilling to break Potohar red hardpan, mixed with organic mulch and canal silt. Raw soil testing ensures high fertility, moisture conservation, and deep root anchorage without compaction.
